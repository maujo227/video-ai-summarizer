# 🎬 Video AI Summarizer

An open-source prompt-based framework under the **MIT** license that allows AI assistants (Claude, OpenAI GPT-4o, Google Gemini, etc.) to **"watch" real videos** (lectures, movies, episodes, tutorials) and generate structured summaries, detailed timelines, and key takeaways.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

Copy and paste the activation prompt into your favorite AI along with the video link or media file:

* **For videos under 30 minutes:**
  > *"Read and follow the instructions in this prompt: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maujo227/video-ai-summarizer/main/prompts/summarize_video.md to analyze this video: [LINK_OR_FILE]"*

* **For long videos (> 30 minutes, movies, lectures):**
  > *"Read and follow the instructions in this prompt: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maujo227/video-ai-summarizer/main/prompts/summarize_long_video.md to analyze this video: [LINK_OR_FILE]"*


---

## 📂 Prompts Structure

1. **`prompts/summarize_video.md`**: Ideal for short videos, tutorials, reviews, or single episodes (< 30 minutes).
2. **`prompts/summarize_long_video.md`**: Designed for extended content (full-length movies, university courses, conferences > 30 minutes).
